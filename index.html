A Statement Regarding Targeted Harassment and Fandom Monitoring

Update, as of 9:00 PM CST on November 6, 2026: the word in Diagon Alley is that The Wizengamont has disbanded and closed their server.

I feel it is necessary to address an ongoing situation involving members of the Harry Potter (HP) fandom and the treatment of my website and myself.

There is a group of individuals within the fandom who operate a Discord server under the name “The Wizengamont.” Based on the information available to me, this group appears to have a specific purpose: monitoring various HP fandom spaces, keeping track of discussions and activity, and reporting information back to their members.

I have become the subject of what I believe to be a coordinated smear campaign involving individuals associated with this group. Rather than engaging with the actual purpose, structure, and content of my website, misinformation and negative narratives have been circulated about both the website and me personally.

For clarity, my website has been in existence for nearly a year. It maintains its own independent database and operates by sharing links to resources. It does not host or distribute live files. There is an important distinction between maintaining a database of links and directly hosting or distributing the files those links may reference, and I believe that distinction deserves to be acknowledged accurately.

People are entitled to their opinions, criticisms, and disagreements. However, there is a difference between legitimate criticism and the deliberate monitoring, targeting, misrepresentation, and spreading of damaging claims about an individual or their work.

I will not accept misinformation being presented as fact, nor will I allow coordinated attempts to damage my reputation or discredit a website that has existed and operated openly for nearly a year.

I am making this statement to establish the facts as I understand them, provide clarity about how my website operates, and make it clear that I am aware of the activity surrounding it.

I have no interest in participating in fandom factionalism, personal vendettas, or coordinated online hostility. My focus remains on my website and the community it serves.

Disagreement is not a justification for harassment, and criticism should never require the distortion of facts.
